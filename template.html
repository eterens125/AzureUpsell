How it’s counted. Upsell orders are orders that used one of the contest coupons. Upsell rate is a team member’s coupon orders divided by all orders they took (the “TM Ord” column in the report). Order counts, not item quantities, are used throughout.

Internet orders are not ranked. Online orders apply coupons on their own, so they are excluded from every ranking and shown only as a memo column in Store Detail. Team members who worked in more than one store are combined into one line on the group board; each store’s own board shows only their orders at that store.
